PRACTICAL USER MANUAL

A clear guide to your Life OS.

Learn what each workspace does, how to start without over-planning, and how to keep your records safe. The system works best when you use only the parts that help.

The short version

Plan a direction, choose the next useful action, record what happened, then adjust your plan using evidence—not guilt or guesswork.

01 · GETTING STARTED

Set up your workspace first

  1. Sign in with your account. This site has no public self-registration. The first account is created securely by the site owner; do not share your credentials.
  2. Start on Today. Review the date and timeline, add up to three priorities, and keep them achievable.
  3. Check your framework. Use Vision 30, Blocks, and Pillars to understand the existing plan. Edit framework details only when you intend to change the plan.
  4. Add your own actions. Use Goals & Tasks for outcomes and next steps, then Habits for repeatable practices. No sample activity is required.
  5. Protect your records. Visit Settings and download a backup after entering information you want to keep. Store that file somewhere private.
First-time account setup

The site owner initializes the database and creates the first superuser outside the website. Once your account exists, sign in from the login page. Do not try to create accounts through the app; it has no public registration form.

02 · A SIMPLE WORKFLOW

Use a small loop you can repeat

01

Orient

Revisit your vision or active block when you need context. You do not need to reread the whole plan every day.

02

Choose

Pick a few meaningful priorities and turn them into clear tasks. A task should describe an action you can actually take.

03

Record

Mark finished tasks and habits, then save a short daily check-in or reflection when it is useful.

04

Review

Look for evidence and patterns. Keep what works, reschedule what still matters, and remove commitments that no longer fit.

A missed day is information, not a verdict. Honest, occasional use is more valuable than maintaining a perfect-looking score.

03 · PAGE REFERENCE

What each workspace is for

DAILY

Today

Your daily workspace: priorities, tasks due today, overdue tasks, eight-pillar check-ins, habits, and an optional evening reflection. Save only what you actually want to record.

OVERVIEW

Dashboard

A high-level snapshot of the framework, active block, timeline, open tasks, and explicitly created measurable targets. It is a summary, not a replacement for the working pages.

DIRECTION

Vision 30

See the overall timeline, master vision, and how the block period fits within the wider date range. Unallocated days and transition time remain visible.

PLANNING

Blocks

Work with the ten planned blocks, their dates, missions, objectives, targets, milestones, and reviews. Open a block to edit its plan and related work.

DEVELOPMENT

Pillars

Explore the eight life areas and their staged work. A pillar can use qualitative evidence, numeric targets, consistency, or a custom scoring approach; there is no single universal life score.

OUTCOMES

Goals & Tasks

Connect desired outcomes to practical next actions. Use a goal for a result you are working toward and a task for a specific action.

ACTIONS

Tasks

Create and manage tasks with a status, priority, optional due date, and relevant pillar, block, or goal. Mark a task complete when it is done; update or reschedule it when plans change.

PRACTICE

Habits

Track repeatable practices. Mark a habit for the current day; toggling it again removes that day's check-in. Streaks reflect consecutive recorded days, not personal worth.

CONTEXT

Life

Read the broader life vision, mission stages, and scorecard context. It helps explain the system's direction alongside the editable planning workspaces.

REFLECTION

Reviews

Save weekly, monthly, or block reviews. Capture wins, challenges, lessons, execution consistency, outcome progress, and next recommendations. Daily reflections are on Today.

NOTES

Journal

Save dated journal entries and optional mood, decision, or pillar details. Entries are private account records; write only what you are comfortable keeping here.

EVIDENCE

Progress

Review recorded execution and outcome measures over time. An empty day is not treated as a zero score, and activity is not the same as an outcome.

MEMORY

Archive

Manually preserve milestones, memories, or other records you want to find later. Completed tasks are not automatically moved here.

PREFERENCES

Settings

Adjust dates and preferences, choose a theme, manage mood/energy options, and export or import a JSON backup. Date changes do not silently rewrite existing block dates.

These workspace links are protected. If you are signed out, Django takes you to the login page and returns you to the requested page after successful sign-in.

04 · DAILY CHECK-IN

A practical start-to-finish session

START

Choose priorities

Add up to three priorities on Today. Use a short outcome or focus, not a long to-do list. Saving the same rank updates that slot for the day.

DO

Work from the action list

Create tasks with a clear next action, status, and optional due date. Tasks due today and overdue incomplete tasks are surfaced on Today.

PRACTICE

Check in across the pillars

For each active pillar, record whether today's practice was completed, partial, or not completed. Optional notes can capture reflection and outcome evidence separately.

CLOSE

Reflect without forcing a score

Save a daily reflection, wins, and an improvement for tomorrow if useful. Mood and energy fields appear only when enabled in Settings.

05 · REVIEWS AND PROGRESS

Separate effort from outcomes

Execution consistency

Captures whether planned actions were completed: tasks, habits, priorities, and daily practices. It helps you see how consistently you followed through.

Outcome progress

Uses goals, measurable targets, and evidence to describe results. Completing many actions does not automatically mean a goal was achieved.

Use weekly reviews to notice near-term patterns, monthly reviews to reconsider direction and commitments, and block reviews to evaluate the plan against evidence. Record recommendations you can act on next. A target is not marked achieved just because a number reaches its limit; review the completion criteria and evidence.

06 · RECORDS AND BACKUPS

Keep your data portable and protected

  • Life OS records are stored in the site's configured database and associated with this deployment—not in browser-only local storage.
  • Sign in from another device to access the same account and database records. Use a trusted device and sign out on shared devices.
  • Settings can export a JSON backup containing framework and personal records. The download may contain sensitive diary entries; store it privately and securely.
  • Import validates a backup, preserves existing personal records, and avoids re-importing an identical file. A Vision 30 backup can update matching framework plans and settings. Review a file before importing it.
  • Legacy browser-dashboard data is not imported automatically. If you have an old export, review its contents and use the Settings import form only when you intend to bring those records into this database.
  • Render's ordinary local filesystem is not a durable media store. This app does not currently persist user-uploaded media files; backup import is processed as a submitted file and should not be treated as long-term file storage.

07 · PRIVACY AND ACCESS

This is a private workspace, not a public journal

The home and guide explain the product without showing diary records. Today, journal, dashboard, plans, reviews, archive, and settings are protected by the existing Django authentication system. The admin page also requires an authenticated admin account.

  • There is no public account-registration page. The site owner creates accounts through the secure administrative process.
  • Never share your password, database URL, secret key, or backup file. Do not store passwords or payment-card details in journal entries.
  • Use the HTTPS site address, sign out from shared devices, and keep your password unique.
  • CSRF protection is enabled for forms. Do not disable browser security controls to work around a failed request.

08 · PHONE AND TABLET

Use the same workspace on a smaller screen

  • Pages adapt to narrow screens; use the Navigation disclosure at the top of the signed-in app to reach its sections.
  • Forms stack into a simpler layout on smaller displays. Scroll within the page rather than zooming out to make a form fit.
  • Use the browser's normal text zoom and a current version of Safari, Chrome, or another standards-compliant browser.
  • Changes are saved by submitting the relevant form. Wait for the page confirmation before closing or navigating away.

09 · TROUBLESHOOTING

Common questions

I clicked a workspace link and got the login page.

That is expected when signed out. Enter your account credentials; after a successful login the app should return you to the requested protected page.

I cannot create an account from the login screen.

Public registration is intentionally not provided. The site owner must create the first account securely; contact them if you do not have credentials.

My older dashboard data is not here.

The previous static dashboard used browser-local data. It is separate from this Django database. Export from the old dashboard if it is still available, inspect the file, then import through Settings if you want those entries here.

Why is there no execution score for a day?

Empty days are not inferred as failures. Record relevant daily work first; progress displays information from days with saved records.

What should I do if a save fails?

Keep the page open and note the error message. Check your connection and required fields, then retry once. For a repeated issue, contact the site owner with the page name and approximate time—never send a password or database URL.
